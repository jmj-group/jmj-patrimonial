Data controller

JMJ Global Patrimonial, S.L.. Contact: hola@jmjpatrimonial.com.

What data we process

This website has no forms and no analytics. We only process the data you send us when you email us: your name, your email address and whatever you choose to tell us.

Purpose and legal basis

To reply to your message and, where relevant, to prepare a working relationship. The legal basis is your consent when you write to us and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(a) and (b) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)).

How long we keep it

For as long as the conversation or relationship lasts and, afterwards, for the periods required by law.

Who we share it with

We do not share your data with third parties unless required by law. Our email is provided by Google and this website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which logs technical visit data such as IP addresses for security purposes.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your data, or to receive it in a portable format, by writing to hola@jmjpatrimonial.com. If you believe your request has not been handled properly, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).
